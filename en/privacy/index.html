Privacy Policy

MetaForm Shot captures and processes photos on your device. The developer does not receive your photos through the app.

Camera, photos, and preferences

Shot uses the camera feed for the viewfinder, filters, and capture. With your permission, it saves finished photos in your photo library. Shot keeps photo references, edits, forms, and themes on your device. It reads the photos you choose from your library and can save edited copies. A capture waiting to be saved is held on your device so you can retry. If an earlier version stored originals inside the app, you can find them under Photo storage in Shot’s settings. When you choose a photo for a theme, Shot copies that image into its local storage.

Location and level

If you enable location tagging and grant location access, Shot records the available capture location in the photo. You can change location access in your device’s settings. The level uses the tilt of your device to show a horizontal guide in the viewfinder.

Saving, sharing, and external services

You choose where to share a photo. Shared files may include the capture location or other metadata, depending on your export settings. Photo library syncing and device backups follow your iCloud and device settings.

Shot has no account registration, advertising SDKs, or developer-operated analytics. GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub handles connection information when you visit the site under its General Privacy Statement.

Contact

If you email support, the developer uses your email address and message to reply and investigate the issue. For privacy questions or deletion requests, contact flames.nagi@gmail.com.
