Help and Support

Save a favorite look and control layout as a form, then recall it when you shoot on iPhone or iPad.

Frequently asked questions

Where do my photos go?

Shot saves each capture in the Photos app. The first time you shoot, allow Shot to add photos. If you previously denied access, tap Open Settings when Shot asks. Select Photos, allow Shot to add photos, and return to Shot. If the capture is waiting to be saved, open its thumbnail and tap Retry saving to Photos. If a save still fails, check the free space on your device.

How do I edit a photo I already have?

On the camera screen, open the camera roll and choose All Photos. Select a photo, adjust its color or crop, then tap Save a copy to Photos to keep the result alongside the original.

How do I save my colors and controls?

Choose a filter and arrange the controls on the camera screen. Tap the current form name, choose Save current layout and color, give your form a name, and tap Save. You can pin up to three forms to the camera screen.

Can I start with the last filter I used?

Turn on Start with last filter in Shot’s settings. With this option off, Shot starts with Original. To return to the color stored in your current form, open the filter panel and tap Restore saved color.

Can I change how I switch forms?

In Shot’s settings, open Form switcher and choose Buttons or List. In list mode, you can also swipe across the form name on the camera screen.

Contact support

Send questions and bug reports to flames.nagi@gmail.com. For a bug report, include your device model, iOS version, Shot version, and the steps that led to the problem. To find your Shot version, open Settings in the app and tap MetaForm Shot.
